About Us

About Lavender Park Beauty Parlour.

Beauty with Care, Comfort & Confidence

Lavender Park Beauty Parlour is a premium beauty salon located on Vembarpatti Main Road, Gopalpatti, Tamil Nadu – 624308. Founded by Anitha Saravanakumar, our salon was established with a simple vision—to provide professional beauty services in a clean, relaxing, and welcoming environment where every client receives dedicated personal attention.

We believe beauty treatments should never feel rushed. Every appointment is carefully scheduled, with one client per chair, allowing our certified beauticians to deliver personalised care using premium-quality products and hygienic practices.

Over the years, Lavender Park Beauty Parlour has become a trusted destination for families across Dindigul and the surrounding areas. From everyday grooming to bridal makeovers and special occasions, we offer complete beauty solutions designed to help you look and feel your best.

Our Mission: make expert beauty care approachable, hygienic, and unhurried.
Our Vision: to be the most trusted beauty studio in the region.
