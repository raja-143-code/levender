Aari Class

Learn Aari Embroidery

Hands-on training in traditional and modern Aari (maggam) embroidery — from your first stitch to bridal-blouse-ready designs — taught in small batches by our in-house artisans.

Beginner to Advanced

Structured levels covering basic chain stitch through mirror, bead and zardosi work.

Bridal Blouse Designs

Practice on real blouse panels — stone work, pearls, and mirror embroidery for wedding wear.

Small Batches

Limited seats per batch so every student gets one-on-one guidance from the instructor.

Certificate on Completion

A completion certificate you can use to start freelance or home-business embroidery work.
